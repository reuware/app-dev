## H2 My Favorite Series
> Tian Guan Ci Fu is a story centers on Xie Lian, the formerly beloved Crown Prince of the Xianle Kingdom, who ascends to godhood for a third time after being banished twice over eight hundred years. Now viewed as a laughingstock and burdened with a heavy debt from accidental property damage in the Heavens, he descends to the mortal realm to exorcise ghosts and spirits. During his travels, he meets San Lang (Hua Cheng), a brilliant and terrifying Ghost King who holds a deep, eternal devotion to him.
